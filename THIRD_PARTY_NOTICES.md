## Font

`assets/fonts/NotoSerifTC.ttf` is Noto Serif TC, downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoseriftc

It is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete copyright notice and license are included in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Its license applies to the font, not automatically to other files in this repository.

## Models, visual effects, and audio

The scene models are generated by `blender/build_scene.py`; shader and UI effects are implemented in this project. The included sound effects and background music are locally synthesized. The background score can be regenerated with `audio/compose_music.py`. No third-party recorded music or instrument samples are bundled.

A privately supplied illustration served as a color reference. That reference image is not redistributed. Approximate sampled colors and subsequent design variations are documented in `PALETTE.md`.
