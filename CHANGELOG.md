# Changelog

## 0.5.0 — Initial public source release

- Three playable chapters with rotating bridges, flower switches, a lift, collectible lights, and completion conditions.
- Portrait framing with orbit, anchored zoom, pan, and gesture support.
- Clearer architectural lighting and a pastel shallow-water background.
- Footstep-triggered ripples that expand and fade around recently crossed structures.
- Fixed coplanar bridge surfaces that caused flickering during rotation.
- Bundled Traditional Chinese serif typography and an original looping instrumental soundtrack.
- Editable Blender source scenes, model generator, verification modes, and a 15-second gameplay showcase.
